Is Jarvis for my kind of business?

Yes, if you own a small business. The three hires do the same jobs in a café, a dental office, a shop or a plumbing company.

What can Jarvis do without asking me?

He reads, drafts, files and reminds. He does not spend, send or delete until you tap.

What happens when I decline what Jarvis drafted?

Nothing goes out, and he keeps the draft.

How long does it take to install Jarvis?

The first month. An Omnia partner does the install with you while you keep running the business.

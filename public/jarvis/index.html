Three kinds of work

That was one day. Most owners hand him one kind of work first.

Right hand

Your chief of staff, by voice. Talk to him by phone, web or iPhone.

  • Knows what needs you before the day starts.
  • Talks a decision through and leaves you one next step.
  • Remembers every conversation and writes in your voice.
  • Keeps your promises, calendar and tasks in one place.
Start with your right hand

Back office

The operation behind the work, from the first message to the money.

  • Turns a conversation into a quote from your prices.
  • Drafts customer replies in your voice and sends them on your tap.
  • Turns an approved quote into an invoice with a pay link.
  • Schedules staff, keeps jobs on the board and chases the paperwork.
Start with the back office

Follow-through

The work that keeps going after you have gone home.

  • Opens the day with a morning brief and ends it with a closing sweep.
  • Chases quiet quotes and overdue invoices on the schedule you set.
  • Runs web errands in a real browser, such as portals, forms and uploads.
  • Holds the fort while you are away and wakes you only for money, a promise or a fire.
Start with follow-through

Getting set up with Omnia

Omnia installs him while you keep running the business. An Omnia partner does the install with you in the first month, then meets you once a month to build the next thing.

  1. Choose the first handoff

    Together we pick the first job for Jarvis: the request coming in, the result you need and the point where you want to review it.

  2. Connect the agreed tools

    We set up your workspace and the connections you agreed to. Your rules, prices and tone shape how he works.

  3. Review the working result

    You check the first result against the way your business runs, and we tune it before anything goes live.

  4. Put it to work

    After that we meet once a month and ship one big build or two small ones. Everything already built keeps running.

He works with what you already use

Google Calendar, Gmail, QuickBooks, Stripe, Slack and Jobber are the usual ones. We check access during the install and agree which connections your build needs. Access follows the scope you approve, and connecting an account does not let him message a customer or move money.

You own what we build

Every tool, connection and piece of data is yours. If you leave, it goes with you.
